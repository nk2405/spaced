Years Running

01

Stargazing Nights

Community telescope sessions to see planets, star clusters and the moon up close.

02

Workshops

Hands-on sessions on astrophotography, rocketry basics, and how to read the night sky.

03

School Outreach

Talks and activities designed for classrooms, introducing space science in an approachable way.

04

Speaker Series

Conversations with students, researchers and professionals working in space and astronomy.

05

Space Literacy Sessions

Beginner-friendly sessions that build core understanding — no prior knowledge needed.

06

Collaborations

Joint events with schools, universities and organizations who share our mission.

OUR JOURNEY

APR 2023

Ideation

The idea for SPACED is born.

JUL 2023

SPACED Instagram launches

SPACED officially begins building its online presence and community.

AUG 2023

Al Quaa 1.0

SPACED hosts its first-ever event, a stargazing night at Al Quaa, Abu Dhabi.

JUN 2024

Al Sadeem

SPACED has an event at Al Sadeem Observatory.

AUG 2024

Al Quaa 2.0

The first Al Quaa event grows into an annual SPACED experience.

AUG 2024

CosmoQuest

SPACED launches CosmoQuest, an exoplanet-focused engagement challenge.

OCT 2024

First NASA Space Apps Team

SPACED enters the NASA Space Apps Challenge for the first time.

NOV 2024

Ready, Set, Uni!

SPACED expands its focus beyond astronomy, helping students explore university and STEM opportunities.

DEC 2024

Geminids Event

SPACED closes out the year with a Geminids meteor shower event.

APR 2025

SPACED Ambassadors Launch

The Ambassador programme begins, creating a wider student network to represent and grow SPACED.

JUN–JUL 2025

Interstellar Mind '25

SPACED collaborates with other organizations for the first Interstellar Mind programme.

JUL 2025

Al Quaa 3.0

The third edition of SPACED's flagship Al Quaa stargazing experience.

AUG 2025

Al Quaa 4.0

SPACED returns to Al Quaa for its fourth edition.

AUG 2025

First Fossil Rock Event

SPACED expands its UAE stargazing programme to a new location at Fossil Rock for Dubai and Sharjah.

OCT 2025

Second NASA Space Apps Team

SPACED returns to NASA Space Apps for its second year.

JUL 2026

Interstellar Mind '26

The second edition of Interstellar Mind launches.

AUG 2026

Al Quaa 5.0

The fifth edition of the Al Quaa stargazing experience.
